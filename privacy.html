Privacy Policy for Open Grammarly

Last Updated: August 03, 2026

Open Grammarly ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome extension handles information.

1. Information Collection

API Keys: We do not require, collect, or store any API keys. The extension works out of the box through a preconfigured DeepSeek proxy.

User Text: When you use the extension's features (grammar checking, improve, rephrase, tone rewrites, or translation), the text from the field you are working on is sent to our DeepSeek proxy endpoint (https://dev.s4m1r.ct.ws) for processing. We do not store this text on our own servers, and no account or personal data is required.

Settings: Your preferences (enable/disable state, writing mode, aggressiveness, last translate target) are stored locally within your browser's secure storage (Chrome Storage API) and never leave your device.

2. Data Usage

The information processed by the extension is used solely to provide the AI writing assistance features. We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data or your processed text to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

This extension uses DeepSeek as its AI provider through a proxy. Your use of the extension is also subject to DeepSeek's Privacy Policy. Please review their policies to understand how they handle data sent to their models.

4. Security

We use the standard Chrome Storage API to save your settings locally. No API keys are stored anywhere, and no personal information is transmitted beyond the text you choose to analyze.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us through our GitHub repository.
